Learning through questions

Research.

Questions behind my projects and the subjects I’m studying. These are directions to explore, rather than published findings.

What should a learning tool understand?

Through Learnly, I’m exploring how AI can support different learning needs. I’m interested in how a tool can respond to confusion and help a student understand the next step.

How much should we trust a prediction?

I’m studying probability and its role in AI, including through Stanford’s Probability for Artificial Intelligence course. I want a stronger mathematical foundation for the systems I build.

What makes a prototype useful outside the lab?

Working on air-quality hardware raised questions about testing, reliability, and everyday use. I’m interested in the gap between an idea that works in a demonstration and one people can rely on.
